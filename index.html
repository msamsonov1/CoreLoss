01

Hysteresis Energy Dissipation & Loop Integration

In high-frequency power magnetic toroids (e.g., Nanoperm, FINEMET $\text{Fe}_{73.5}\text{Si}_{13.5}\text{B}_9\text{Nb}_3\text{Cu}_1$), the magnetic energy dissipated as heat per switching period $T_{\text{sw}}$ is the line integral of magnetic field strength $H$ over flux density $B$, directly converted to digital telemetry via integrated terminal flux linkage $\Phi$:

$$E_{\text{diss}} = V_c \oint H\,dB = \frac{V_c}{N_{\text{sec}}} \oint B(t)\, d\Phi(t)$$

Here $V_c$ is effective magnetic core volume and $N_{\text{sec}}$ is the number of turns on the sentry pickoff winding. Total high-frequency core loss per unit volume incorporates the modified Steinmetz expansion:

$$P_v = C_{\text{SE}} f_{\text{sw}}^\alpha \Delta B^\beta \cdot \left[1 + \gamma_{\text{rad}}(T) \cdot \Phi_{\text{SEP}}\right]$$
02

Microscopic Radiation-Induced Domain Pinning

Solar Energetic Particle (SEP) events unleash relativistic protons ($E_p > 10\text{ MeV}$) and iron heavy ions ($^{56}\text{Fe}$). Primary knock-on atoms (PKA) generate vacancy clusters and dislocation loops across the 10 nm amorphous grain boundaries, creating microscopic magnetic pinning sites:

$$H_c(\Phi_{\text{SEP}}) = H_{c0} + \Delta H_c = H_{c0} + \kappa_{\text{pinning}} \frac{\sqrt{N_d \cdot r_d}}{\mu_0 M_s}$$

Where $N_d$ is radiation defect cascade density and $M_s$ is spontaneous saturation magnetization. Pinning widens coercivity $H_c$, ballooning the phase loop and multiplying losses by up to $340\%$.

03

Thermal Runaway Positive Feedback Loop

Nanocrystalline materials exhibit a sharp Curie temperature $T_c \approx 570^\circ\text{C}$, but effective saturation flux density $B_{\text{sat}}(T)$ degrades precipitously beyond $120^\circ\text{C}$ according to the Bloch $T^{3/2}$ law:

$$B_{\text{sat}}(T) = B_{\text{sat}}(0) \left[1 - s \left(\frac{T}{T_c}\right)^{3/2}\right]$$

When radiation increases $E_{\text{diss}}$, core heat generation outstrips spacecraft radiator dissipation. $B_{\text{sat}}$ collapses into the operating flux swing $\Delta B$, driving inductor permeability to vacuum levels $\mu_0$, causing catastrophic current spikes that destroy primary GaN/SiC FETs.

04

Real-Time Inversion & Autonomous Mode Adaptation

The CoreLoss embedded sentry samples pickoff coil voltage $v_s(t) = -N_s \frac{d\Phi}{dt}$ at 20 MSPS. A dedicated DSP executes trapezoidal discrete contour integration every switching cycle:

$$\mathcal{A}_k = \sum_{n=1}^{M} \frac{B_n + B_{n-1}}{2} \cdot (\Phi_n - \Phi_{n-1})$$

If $\mathcal{A}_k > \mathcal{A}_{\text{threshold}}$, the FPGA issues instantaneous modulation commands: throttling switching frequency $f_{\text{sw}}$ ($350\text{ kHz} \to 120\text{ kHz}$) and transitioning gate drives to Zero-Voltage-Switching (ZVS) Quasi-Resonant mode.
